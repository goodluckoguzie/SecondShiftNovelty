Proposed product · UK dementia care at home

Second Shift

Voice-first AI for unpaid family carers. Turns two weeks of exhaustion into one page of evidence for the GP.

Closed loop

1. Voice at home

Carer speaks naturally at 11pm. No forms. No tapping through menus.

→
2. Structured log

Speech becomes typed events: medicine, confusion, meal, sleep, mood.

→
3. Pattern engine

Rules flag late doses, recurring confusion, and clusters after a dose change.

→
4. GP / memory-clinic brief

One-page PDF. Every line cites the voice-log timestamp it came from.
